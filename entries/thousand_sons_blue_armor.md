# Thousand Sons Blue Armor

## Recipe

- **Paint:** Scale75 Adriatic Blue
- **Mix (drops):**
  - 20x thinner
  - 10x paint
  - 2x flow improver
- **Airbrush:** Neo Eco
- **Needle:** 0.5mm
- **Pressure:** 25 PSI
